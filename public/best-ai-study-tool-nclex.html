NCLEX Question Types We Generate

The NCLEX exam uses various question formats to assess clinical judgment and nursing knowledge. NurseQuizAI generates all major question types so you're fully prepared:

  • Select All That Apply (SATA): These challenging questions require you to identify multiple correct nursing interventions or assessments. Our AI generates SATA questions with detailed rationales explaining why each option should or shouldn't be selected. Learn more about SATA question strategies.
  • Prioritization Questions: Which patient do you see first? What's the priority nursing action? These questions test your ability to triage and make critical decisions under pressure.
  • Clinical Judgment Questions: The Next-Gen NCLEX emphasizes clinical judgment through case studies, extended multiple response, and cloze (dropdown) questions. NurseQuizAI prepares you for these newer formats.
  • Delegation & Assignment: Questions about what can be delegated to LPNs, UAPs, or other team members - critical knowledge for real-world nursing practice.
  • Medication Calculations: Drug dosage problems requiring mathematical accuracy and nursing judgment about safe administration.
  • Standard Multiple Choice: Traditional four-option questions covering nursing theory, anatomy, pharmacology, and patient care.

Every question includes comprehensive rationales that explain not just the correct answer, but why each incorrect option is wrong. This helps build the clinical reasoning skills you need for both NCLEX and real patient care.
