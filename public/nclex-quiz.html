Prioritization

A nurse receives report on four clients. Which client should the nurse assess FIRST?

  • A A client with COPD and oxygen saturation of 91% on 2L nasal cannula
  • B A client 1 day post-appendectomy with pain rated 6/10
  • C A client with diabetes mellitus and a blood glucose of 58 mg/dL ✓ Correct
  • D A client with heart failure who gained 2 pounds overnight

💡 Rationale

Hypoglycemia is an immediate life-threatening emergency. A blood glucose of 58 mg/dL can rapidly progress to seizures, loss of consciousness, and death if not treated immediately...
