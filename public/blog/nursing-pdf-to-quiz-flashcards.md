You don’t have time to hand-build decks. Here’s a faster way to turn nursing PDFs and slides into NCLEX-style study materials.

## The upload-to-quiz workflow
1) **Upload** PDFs, lecture decks, or images.
2) **Insights**: Auto-detected topics and localized titles (English/French).
3) **NCLEX quiz**: Includes “select all that apply” to mirror exam pressure.
4) **Flashcards & study sheets**: Instant cards and printable sheets from the same files.
5) **Audio summaries**: Listen while commuting or on break.

## Why this beats manual decks
- No copy/paste; uploads drive everything.
- Questions match *your* lectures, not generic banks.
- Multi-format output: quiz → flashcards → sheet → audio.
- Bilingual outputs follow your browser language.

## Pro tips
- Batch upload a week of slides; generate a quiz, then export weak spots to flashcards.
- Use study sheets to condense patho, meds, labs, and interventions before clinicals.
- Switch browser language to French for fully localized titles and summaries.
