Question 1 · Multiple choice
A client has pulmonary tuberculosis. Which precautions should the nurse use?
  1. Contact
  2. Droplet
  3. Airborne
  4. Standard only
Show answer and rationale

Answer: C

A. Contact precautions are for infections spread by touch.

B. Droplet is for larger particles like influenza.

C. Correct. TB needs a negative-pressure room and an N95 respirator.

D. Standard precautions alone are not enough.

Question 2 · Multiple choice
A client has Clostridioides difficile. How should the nurse clean their hands after care?
  1. Alcohol-based hand rub
  2. Soap and water
  3. Either is acceptable
  4. Gloves alone are enough
Show answer and rationale

Answer: B

A. Alcohol does not kill C. diff spores.

B. Correct. Soap and water physically remove spores.

C. Only soap and water is effective here.

D. Hand hygiene is required after glove removal.

Question 3 · Select all that apply
Which conditions require airborne precautions? Select all that apply.
  1. Measles
  2. Varicella (chickenpox)
  3. Influenza
  4. Tuberculosis
  5. MRSA wound infection
Show answer and rationale

Answer: A, B, D

A. Correct.

B. Correct. Chickenpox needs airborne and contact precautions.

C. Influenza uses droplet precautions.

D. Correct.

E. MRSA uses contact precautions.

Question 4 · Multiple choice
What is the correct order for removing PPE?
  1. Mask, gown, gloves, goggles
  2. Gloves, goggles or face shield, gown, mask
  3. Gown, mask, gloves, goggles
  4. Goggles, mask, gloves, gown
Show answer and rationale

Answer: B

A. Removing the mask first contaminates the face with dirty gloves.

B. Correct. Gloves (most contaminated) first, mask last, then hand hygiene.

C. Gloves should come off first.

D. Gloves are the most contaminated and come off first.

Question 5 · Select all that apply
Which actions maintain a sterile field? Select all that apply.
  1. Keep sterile items above waist level
  2. Turn your back to the field briefly to get supplies
  3. Consider the outer 1 inch of the field non-sterile
  4. Pour solutions without splashing the field
  5. Reach across the sterile field
Show answer and rationale

Answer: A, C, D

A. Correct. Below the waist is considered contaminated.

B. A field out of view is considered contaminated.

C. Correct.

D. Correct. Moisture wicks contamination through the field.

E. Reaching across contaminates the field.

How to study infection control for the NCLEX

  1. Airborne: "My Chicken Hez TB" (measles, chickenpox, herpes zoster disseminated, TB).
  2. Droplet: influenza, pertussis, meningitis, mumps; mask within about 3 feet.
  3. C. diff means soap and water every time.

Memorise the short airborne and droplet lists; most other common infections in exam questions use contact precautions.
