# Face-on-camera scripts

From DarkUGC research (Nov 2026). In its education library, every top video
shows a face, the winning hooks are "relatable stress" and "regret" rather than
quiz questions, and the best length is under 30 seconds. NursifyAI's top three
nursing videos (4.6M, 4.2M, 1.4M views) came from one creator with 627
followers, filming herself on her couch with one line of text on screen.

These are patterns, not copies: same structure, our own words.

Each takes under a minute to film on a phone. No talking needed: text on
screen plus a trending sound. Film yourself, or send these to student creators.

---

## 1. "The bonus section" (relatable stress, lip-sync)
**Shot:** you on the couch, dramatic face, mouthing along to a trending sound. One take, 8 to 12 s.
**On-screen text:**
> wish exams had a section for "everything i studied that wasn't on here"
> (i made 200 practice questions from my lecture slides 😭)

**Caption:** the way i KNEW the other 199 #nursingstudent #nursingschool #nclex #studytok

## 2. "How I studied / how I study now" (problem → app answer, 9 s)
**Shot 1 (0 to 4 s):** highlighting the textbook, then picking up your phone and scrolling.
Text: `how i studied for pharm`
**Shot 2 (4 to 9 s):** screen recording of uploading slides to NurseQuizAI, then answering a question.
Text: `how i study now`
**Caption:** my highlighter was doing nothing 💀 #nursingschool #studytok #pharmacology

## 3. "Last semester and I just found this" (regret + screen demo, 12 s)
**Shot 1:** you at a desk, hand on forehead.
Text: `final semester of nursing school and i JUST found this 😭`
**Shot 2:** screen recording, with one text card per step:
- `upload your lecture slides`
- `NCLEX-style questions from YOUR notes`
- `select all that apply too 🤯`
- `tells you what you keep missing`
**Shot 3:** you looking locked in. Text: `it's called NurseQuizAI`

## 4. "POV: exam is tomorrow" (POV, 10 s)
**Shot:** 11 pm, lamp on, notes everywhere, you staring at the camera.
Text: `pov: your med-surg exam is in 10 hours and you haven't started`
Cut to the screen: the "Tonight's plan" rounds. Text: `this is how i'm passing`

## 5. "Things nursing school didn't tell me" (list, 15 s)
**Shot:** walking or sitting, each line a new text card:
- `nobody tells you SATA is all or nothing`
- `nobody tells you to practise on YOUR instructor's slides`
- `nobody tells you there's an app that turns them into questions`

---

## Where the faceless quiz videos fit
The rendered quiz videos in `out/` are the easy daily filler: they build
comments ("I picked B!") and cost you nothing. The face videos above are the
ones with a chance of breaking out. Aim for about 3 face videos a week on top
of the daily quiz.

## If you pay creators
NursifyAI's numbers say follower count barely matters: a 627-follower student
reached 4.6M. Find nursing students with 500 to 10k followers who post their
own studying, send them scripts 1 to 3, and pay per post or per signup.
