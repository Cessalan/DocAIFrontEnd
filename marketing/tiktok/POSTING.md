# Week 1 posts

Videos are in `marketing/tiktok/out/`. Each is 20 seconds, 1080x1920, no sound.

## How to post (about 5 minutes each)
1. In TikTok, tap **+**, then upload the MP4.
2. Tap **Sounds** and pick a trending sound at low volume. Upbeat, no lyrics works best behind text.
3. Paste the caption below.
4. Post the same file to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.
5. In the first hour, reply to comments. Pin one that asks "what did you pick?".

Post one a day, around 7 to 9 pm in your audience's time zone (US Eastern/Central), when students study.
