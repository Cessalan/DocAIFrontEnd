# Daily NCLEX Question posts

Finished videos are in `out/` (re-render with `node marketing/tiktok/render-daily.js`).
The old designed quiz cards (`out/01-*.mp4` to `07-*.mp4`) are retired: do not post them.

## How to post
1. Upload the MP4 to TikTok, add a trending sound at low volume.
2. Paste the caption below.
3. After about an hour of comments, reply with the answer and pin that reply.
4. Post the same file to Instagram Reels.
